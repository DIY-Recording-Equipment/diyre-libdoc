Most THRU Cable problems show up as a power issue somewhere downstream in the system it's connecting, rather than in the cable itself. The sections below are ordered by likelihood, so work down from the top.

## General resources

- [THRU Cable Assembly Guide](https://www.diyrecordingequipment.com/pages/thru-cable-assembly-guide)
- [General Troubleshooting Tips](/docs/troubleshooting/general/general-troubleshooting-tips/)

## A connected unit powers up briefly then shuts down, or the external PSU flashes blue instead of staying solid {.step}

1. Disconnect the THRU Cable and check whether the affected unit (a Colour Duo, G Bus, or PR502, for example) powers up normally on its own supply. If it does, the fault is more likely with the power chain the THRU Cable is part of than the cable itself.
2. If the 48V light is flickering along with this symptom, check that connector for a loose seat at both ends of the THRU Cable.
3. If the issue persists with the THRU Cable removed entirely, troubleshoot the affected unit's own power section using its product-specific guide.

## Stripped a wire and it pulled back through the cable jacket {.step}

If you're replacing a damaged wire, match the gauge and type used in the original cable rather than substituting whatever's on hand. Using a mismatched wire spec can affect fit inside the jacket and connector.
