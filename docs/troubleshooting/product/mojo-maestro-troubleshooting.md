## No signal {.step}

1. Check for cold or incomplete solder joints. See [Cleaning Up Solder Joints](https://support.diy.re/article/20-cleaning-up-solder-joints).
2. Confirm ICs are inserted in the right orientation.
3. Double check all resistor values.
4. Check the orientation of all diodes.
