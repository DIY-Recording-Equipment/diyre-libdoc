## Very quiet signal {.step}

Ribbon mics are inherently low-output, and the RM-5/RM-6 may need 60dB or more of clean gain from your preamp to sound right. If your signal is quiet but clean, this is often just normal operation rather than a fault.

## Hum or ground noise {.step}

1. Check continuity between the XLR pin 1 and the mic body with a multimeter.
2. Check for a loose XLR-mount screw or a missed pin 3/4 jumper. A bad or incomplete ground connection here, rather than the mic itself, is the most common cause.
3. Re-solder the ground jumper if it looks incomplete.

## Ribbon broke during assembly or use {.step}

The ribbon is extremely fragile. Common causes during assembly include breathing on it while soldering nearby, pliers slipping into the magnet gap, or a stray washer getting pulled into the motor magnet. If your ribbon is broken, please request a new one via the **[Parts Request](/docs/contact/parts/)** form.

## End cap won't seat, or seems misaligned {.step}

1. The end cap fits snugly by design and often need gentle prying with a screwdriver to seat.
2. If it still won't seat, check that the motor is attached to the cap in the correct orientation. A motor attached backward to the cap will prevent it from seating no matter how much force you use.
