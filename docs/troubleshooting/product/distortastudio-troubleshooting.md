## Volume or gain drops 10 to 20dB when the module is engaged {.step}

Double check all resistor values. In nearly every reported case, this symptom traces to these two resistors being swapped on the board. Desoldering and correctly placing them resolves it.

## Distortion is higher than expected {.step}

This is most likely normal. The Distortastudio runs a very high default distortion and output level compared to gentler Colour modules. If you would like to reduce the amount of distortion, try the [Distortastudio less distortion mod](/docs/mods/distortastudio-less-distortion/).

## Sound is muffled/low-pass-filtered {.step}

Double check the values of all capacitors to make sure you haven't swapped any of the 47p and .1u caps.
