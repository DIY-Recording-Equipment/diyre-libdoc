Nearly every 500EXT issue traces back to the ribbon cable or card-edge connector going in backward or misaligned at one end. The sections below are ordered by likelihood, so work down from the top.

## General resources

- [500EXT Assembly Guide](https://www.diyrecordingequipment.com/pages/500ext-assembly-guide)
- [General Troubleshooting Tips](/docs/troubleshooting/general/general-troubleshooting-tips/)

## 1. No continuity, or connections seem reversed at one end

Check the IDC ribbon cable and card-edge connector orientation, paying close attention to pin 1 alignment on both ends. Soldering the ribbon cable or connector backward is the most common cause of a 500EXT that doesn't pass signal correctly. Test continuity between the boards pin-by-pin with a multimeter to confirm.

## 2. Partial or no voltage on the ±16V or 48V rails when testing a module through the 500EXT

This almost always traces back to the same soldering or orientation mistake as Section 1 rather than a defective extender board. Go to Section 1 and check the ribbon cable and connector orientation.
