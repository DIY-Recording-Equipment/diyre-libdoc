If the output of your SB2 is uneven between channels, check the following:

1. Are the dots on the resistors arrays lined up with the corresponding dots on the circuit board?
2. Are both resistors R5 and R6 [soldered well](/troubleshooting/cleaning-up-solder-joints/)?
3. If you have a multi-meter you can check your build by confirming the resistance between the points in this chart: [SB2 Resistance Testing Values (Google Sheets)](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1BeqA4_xBLnzx1VZ9KrdT6MJ01Qyhc9CvU8hx19foHd4/edit?usp=sharing).
