International orders are processed through Shopify Managed Markets. For every international region we ship to, **import duties and taxes are included in the price you see at checkout**. You won't be asked to pay anything extra to customs or the carrier when your package arrives.

Here's how it breaks down by region:

| Region | Import duties | Taxes (VAT/GST) | Paid on delivery? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| United States | None (domestic) | Sales tax added at checkout where applicable | No |
| Canada | Included in price | Included in price (GST/HST) | No |
| Europe & UK | Included in price | Included in price (VAT) | No |
| All other international countries | Included in price | Included in price, based on your country | No |

**Why do prices look different in my country?**

Because duties and local taxes are built into international prices, the price you see may be higher than the US price. That difference covers the import costs you would otherwise pay to your customs agency on delivery.

**A carrier is asking me to pay import fees.**

You shouldn't be charged anything on delivery. If a carrier or customs agency asks you for duties or taxes on one of our orders, please don't pay it. [Contact us](/docs/contact/) with your order number and we'll sort it out.

**Can you change the value on the customs form?**

No. The customs paperwork is generated automatically from your order, and we can't change it, sorry!
